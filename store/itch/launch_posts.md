Post (put the GIF right after the second paragraph):

```text
Limpid Chess is out, and you can play it right here in your browser, on a computer or a phone.

It's a smooth chess game built around one idea: a blank board with thirty legal moves makes a lot of us freeze. So on every turn, Limpid Chess draws just three moves on the board, all in the same color. One is the best move, one is "not bad", and one is a blunder. Your job is to find the best one.

How a turn works
1. Look at the three arrows. No hints, no labels.
2. Pick one. The colors reveal how you did: a green check for the best move, a blue dash for "not bad", a red cross for the blunder.
3. Get your grade and see what the best move was. Mistakes are the lesson, never a punishment.

Little by little, you start seeing the good moves before the arrows show them.

What's inside
• Friendly bots built on Stockfish, dialed down so beginners can actually win
• Puzzles: a rising streak of real tactics from the Lichess puzzle database, each one still shown as three moves. One wrong move ends the run
• A move-by-move review after each game, to see where it turned
• 13 languages, all in the dyslexia-friendly OpenDyslexic font, and a symbol on every revealed move so colors are never needed
• No ads, no account, no clock

Free in your browser
Here on itch.io you get 3 games a day against 7 bots (plus 2 bonus games to start), a daily Puzzles run (plus 2 bonus runs) and a daily game review. Nothing is sold here. If you want it all, Premium is a one-time purchase in the Android app or on the website: unlimited games, Puzzles and reviews, all 14 bots, and Face to Face for two players on one device.

Free software
Limpid Chess is open source under GPL-3.0, like the Stockfish engine inside it: https://github.com/Hatscat/limpidchess
Made with Godot by Lone Bee, a one-person studio. The three-move idea was inspired by Lazy Chess.

I'd love your feedback, especially if you're new to chess: which bot felt fair, which moves surprised you, anything that got in your way. Comments are open below, and if you'd like to hear about my next games, you can follow Lone Bee.

Also by Lone Bee: Draw my Code, a daily puzzle where you read a tiny C function and paint its output. https://lone-bee.itch.io/draw-my-code
```
